# TIME RIFT — Player's Guide

A sneaky time villain broke history into pieces! Jump through glowing time
rifts, land in the past, and fix each era by solving puzzles. Good luck,
time traveler.

## Getting started

1. Open the game. Pick your explorer name, or make a new one with
   **+ New Explorer**.
2. You'll land on the **Rift Map** — a path through history. Each glowing
   circle is an era you can visit. Locked ones (with a 🔒) need you to
   finish the one before them first. Grayed-out "Coming soon" ones aren't
   built yet.
3. Tap an unlocked era to jump through the portal!

## Playing an era

Every era has its own puzzle — not a quiz, a real challenge:

- **Ancient Egypt** 🔺 — Each giant stone block has a problem (stuck in a
  gap, needs to go up a steep slope...). Tap the tool that actually solves
  that problem: Ramp, Lever, Sledge, or Wet Sand.
- **Ancient Rome** 🏛️ — A vault lock shows you a number. Tap the Roman
  numeral letters (I, V, X, L, C, D, M) in the right order to spell that
  number, then hit **Open Lock**.
- **Viking Seas** ⛵ — Drag inside the compass dial to point your ship's
  bow the right direction, using Polaris (the North Star) as your guide.
  Then tap **Set Course!**.
- **Moon Landing** 🚀 — Pick an engine and slide the fuel tank to get your
  rocket both (1) strong enough to lift off and (2) carrying enough fuel to
  reach orbit. Too much fuel makes it too heavy to lift off — too little
  and it runs out before reaching orbit!

Getting something wrong never ends the game — you just learn why and try
again.

## Hints, stars, and mute

- Stuck? Tap **Hint 💡** in the top bar. Using a hint is totally fine, but
  it does cost you one star for that era (you can always replay for more
  stars later).
- Finishing an era earns you **1 to 3 stars** ⭐⭐⭐ based on how few
  mistakes and hints you needed and how fast you went.
- Tap the 🔊 / 🔇 icon anytime to mute or unmute sound.

## After each era

You'll see how many stars you earned, a cool artifact you collected for
your **Museum**, and a short "what really happened" card with the real
history or science behind the puzzle you just solved.

## The Museum

Tap **🏺 Museum** on the Rift Map to see every artifact you've found. Mystery
boxes (❔) are artifacts you haven't unlocked yet — go finish that era!

## Playing with family or friends

Tap **🔄 Switch** on the Rift Map to see all the explorer profiles on this
device. Everyone gets their own stars, artifacts, and progress — nothing
gets mixed up.

## Playing offline

Once you've opened TIME RIFT in your browser one time, it keeps working
even without internet! On an iPhone, tap the Share button in Safari, then
"Add to Home Screen," to put it right on your home screen like a real app.
